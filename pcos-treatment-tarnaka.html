FAQs — Tarnaka Patients

What are PCOS symptoms?
Irregular or absent periods, difficulty conceiving, excess facial/body hair, acne, weight gain, thinning hair, dark skin patches, and polycystic ovaries on ultrasound.
Can PCOS be cured?
PCOS cannot be cured, but is very well manageable. With lifestyle changes and medical treatment, most symptoms improve significantly.
Can I get pregnant with PCOS near Tarnaka?
Yes — PCOS is one of the most treatable causes of infertility. Many PCOS women conceive with ovulation induction tablets or IUI.
What is the difference between PCOS and PCOD?
PCOD and PCOS are the same condition — PCOS is the internationally accepted medical term.
