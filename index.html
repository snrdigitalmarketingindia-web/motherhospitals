How IVF Works

Your IVF Journey in Five Steps

What happens in a typical IVF cycle, from the first medicines to the embryo transfer.

  1. Stimulation

    Medicines help several eggs grow in the ovaries, usually for 10 to 12 days. Regular scans track the follicles.

    How IVF works →

  2. Egg retrieval

    A short procedure under sedation. A fine needle, guided by ultrasound, collects the eggs.

    About egg retrieval →

  3. Fertilisation

    In the lab, eggs meet sperm, either by IVF or by ICSI, where one sperm is placed into each egg.

    About ICSI →

  4. Embryo growth

    Embryos grow in the lab for 3 to 5 days. The embryologist checks their development every day.

    About blastocysts →

  5. Embryo transfer

    The chosen embryo is placed in the uterus with a soft, thin tube. A pregnancy test follows about two weeks later.

    About the transfer →

Pregnancy, Trimester by Trimester

How your baby grows, and the key scans at each stage.

Weeks 1–13

First trimester

The heart, brain and organs begin to form. A dating scan confirms the pregnancy, and the NT scan is done at 11 to 14 weeks.

About the NT scan →
Weeks 14–27

Second trimester

Your baby grows quickly and you begin to feel movements. The anomaly scan at 18 to 22 weeks checks the baby in detail.

About the anomaly scan →
Weeks 28–40

Third trimester

Your baby gains weight and usually turns head-down. Growth scans and regular check-ups help plan a safe delivery.
